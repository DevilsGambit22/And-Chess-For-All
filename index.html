Created in Memory of Brandon

DevilsGambit22 began playing chess casually in 2019. In 2022, after meeting his late friend Brandon, he became more dedicated to studying and competing in the game.

And Chess For All was created in Brandon's memory with the hope of sharing chess knowledge, encouraging friendly competition, and helping players build lasting friendships through a game that brings people together across all backgrounds and skill levels.
